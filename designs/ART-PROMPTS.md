- DALL·E can't do transparent backgrounds; that's fine — keep the **plain dark background** and I'll drop each image into the card frame (the frame already supplies the name, lore, and border).
- **Save each image** named exactly like the tag shown (e.g. `art_Te.png` for a compound, `art_WOLVERINE.png` for a stack) and drop them in `designs/mythic_art/`. Then tell me and I'll wire them into the cards automatically.
